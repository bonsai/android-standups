# Research Seminar: What Should We Research?

## Format

Research results and prior studies are brought into a seminar. The student first performs self-questioning, then receives objections from peers and specialists, followed by professor-level review.

## Dialogue

### Student
What should we actually research?

### Professor
Do not start with humor generation. First determine why humans judge a deviation as funny.

### Student
My hypothesis is that humor may function as a detector of the boundary of an established order.

### Theory Senior
That sounds close to incongruity theory. What is new?

### Student
The proposed contribution is not incongruity itself. It is an explicit chain from world knowledge to order, premise, expectation, expected/actual states, and a measurable deviation representation.

### NLP Senior
Can an AI infer the expectation reliably?

### Student
That should become an empirical question rather than an assumption.

### Cognitive Science Senior
Unexpectedness is not the same as humor. How will you distinguish them?

### Student
Measure surprise, weirdness, understandability, and humor separately while controlling deviation.

### Statistics Senior
Can deviation be manipulated experimentally?

### Student
Construct controlled examples with increasing deviation and compare human judgments against semantic-distance baselines.

### Comedy Senior
A large deviation can become meaningless rather than funny.

### Professor
Then the boundary between unexpectedness and humor is the research target.

## Decision

The immediate research target is not "write funny scripts". It is:

> Identify, represent, and measure the boundary between unexpectedness and humor.

Generation is a later application of the evaluator.

## Research direction

```text
Prior Research
  -> Seminar
  -> Self-Question
  -> Objection
  -> Counterargument
  -> Research Gap
  -> Theory
  -> Planning
```

## Open questions

- How should order and expectation be operationalized?
- Which deviation dimensions matter?
- Is deviation more explanatory than semantic distance?
- Does humor peak at an intermediate deviation range?
- How should cultural and contextual knowledge be handled?
- Can LLM evaluation reproduce human judgments?
