# 06 — Lab Drinking-Party Conversation: Research Observation

## Status
Working hypothesis generated from informal discussion. Not a research result.

## Scene
A lab conversation in which researchers discuss AI, research, failure, and everyday topics.

## Observation
Humor may emerge from a local deviation from shared premises, expectations, and immediate conversational context.

Example:

```text
A: AI wrote the paper.
B: Then let AI review it.
C: Then let AI review the AI review.
```

The conversational order escalates until the structure itself becomes the object of humor.

## Working hypothesis
Conversation humor may depend on a locally recognizable expectation and a small but interpretable deviation from it.

## Candidate ontology

```text
Conversation
├── Participant
├── Shared Knowledge
├── Local Context
├── Norm
├── Expectation
├── Utterance
├── Deviation
│   ├── semantic
│   ├── contextual
│   ├── role

│   └── escalation
├── Recognition
└── Humor Response
```

## Important distinction

```text
unexpected ≠ funny

unexpected + interpretable
        ↓
possible humor
```

## Experiment idea
Manipulate deviation while holding the conversation context constant:

```text
baseline
small deviation
medium deviation
large deviation
incoherent
```

Measure human humor judgments and compare deviation with funny score.

## Scope
Use as a hypothesis source for stand-up, manzai, conversation, and human-android dialogue research.
