# Research Seminar Framework

## Purpose
Use a research-seminar dialogue to stress-test prior research, hypotheses, novelty, methods, evaluation, and experimental design before planning implementation.

## Human-facing rule
Human records are Japanese meeting minutes. Preserve conclusions, objections, decisions, unresolved questions, and action items.

## LLM-facing rule
The internal research layer is English-only Markdown. No Python implementation is required at this stage.

## Seminar roles

### Student
Present claims, defend the research gap, and revise hypotheses when evidence requires it.

### Theory Senior
Challenge theoretical novelty, definitions, and falsifiability.

### NLP Senior
Challenge computational validity, LLM dependence, ontology operationalization, and baselines.

### Cognitive Science Senior
Challenge the human interpretation model and the distinction between surprise and humor.

### Statistics Senior
Challenge variables, sample size, baselines, measurement, and statistical identifiability.

### Comedy / Domain Senior
Challenge ecological validity across stand-up, manzai, dialogue, and everyday conversation.

### Experimental Senior
Challenge reproducibility, controlled manipulation, annotation, and evaluator separation.

### Professor
Review research question, novelty, falsifiability, operational definitions, design, evidence, limitations, and publication value.

## Standard sequence

```text
Paper Selection
 → Student Summary
 → Claim Extraction
 → Method Inspection
 → Evidence Inspection
 → Limitation Extraction
 → Theory Critique
 → NLP Critique
 → Cognitive Critique
 → Statistics Critique
 → Domain Critique
 → Experimental Critique
 → Professor Review
 → Research Gap
 → Hypothesis Revision
 → Planning Request
```

## Objection protocol

```text
ACCEPT
REVISE
REJECT
EXPERIMENT_REQUIRED
```

Preserve objections as research history.

## Argument structure

```text
Claim
 → Evidence
 → Objection
 → Counterargument
 → Decision
 → Planning Consequence
```

## Human review policy
Human review is sparse and deliberate. Intervene at major hypothesis changes, ontology changes, planning approval, surprising results, final interpretation, and publication decisions.
