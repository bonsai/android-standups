# Recap of Lab — Research Discussion

## Purpose
Use conversational discussion to make research understandable to humans while preserving objections and decisions as an internal review process.

### Student
"Humor may be a detector of the boundary of an established order."

### Senior
"Isn't that already covered by incongruity theory?"

### Student
"The novelty candidate is not incongruity itself. It is an explicit world-knowledge → order → premise → expectation → deviation representation."

### Professor
"Then show whether that representation explains human humor judgments better than semantic distance alone."

### Senior
"And don't treat an LLM judge as ground truth."

### Student
"Human evaluation remains the calibration reference."

### Professor
"Good. Convert the argument into an experiment."

## Seminar model

```text
Prior Research
      ↓
Student Summary
      ↓
Senior Critique
      ↓
Professor Review
      ↓
Research Gap
      ↓
Hypothesis Revision
      ↓
Experiment Proposal
```

## Seminar principle

Research discussion is an internal peer-review mechanism, not a performance script.

## Critical questions

- What differs from incongruity theory?
- What does Deviation Vector explain beyond semantic distance?
- How are world knowledge, premise, and expectation operationalized?
- How should LLM-human disagreement be handled?
- How can the hypothesis be falsified?
- How is humor separated from mere surprise?
