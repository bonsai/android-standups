# 04 — Computational Humour Generation and Explanation

## Citation
Loakman, Thorne & Lin (2025), *Who's Laughing Now? An Overview of Computational Humour Generation and Explanation*.

## URL
https://aclanthology.org/2025.inlg-main.45/

## PDF
https://aclanthology.org/2025.inlg-main.45.pdf

## Research role
Review of computational humor generation and explanation, including LLMs, evaluation, and common-sense knowledge.

## Relevance
Provides the research background for explaining why something is funny to both AI systems and humans.

## Project gap
Structure the explanation as:

```text
World Knowledge → Order → Premise → Expectation → Deviation
```

rather than relying only on free-form LLM explanations.

## Project extension
Store explanations as machine-readable Ontology, MicroSkills, and Deviation Vectors.
