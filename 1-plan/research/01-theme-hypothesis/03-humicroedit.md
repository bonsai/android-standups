# 03 — Humicroedit

## Citation
Hossain, Krumm & Gamon (2019), *President Vows to Cut <Taxes> Hair: Dataset and Analysis of Creative Text Editing for Humorous Headlines*.

## URL
https://aclanthology.org/N19-1012/

## PDF
https://aclanthology.org/N19-1012.pdf

## Dataset
https://www.cs.rochester.edu/u/nhossain/humicroedit.html

## Research role
Human-evaluated humor dataset and controlled text-editing experimental model.

## Core idea
News headlines are edited to introduce humor. The dataset contains 15,095 examples with multiple human ratings.

## Relevance
Provides a close precedent for manipulating deviation and measuring its relationship with human humor judgments.

## Project extension
```text
Original
 → Deviation 0.1
 → Deviation 0.2
 → Deviation 0.3
 → Human preference / funny score
```

Extend beyond word-level edits to scripts, dialogue, situations, and social/world-knowledge constraints.
