# 02 — Incongruity-Resolution Cases in Jokes

## Citation
Yus (2017), *Incongruity-resolution cases in jokes*.

## URL
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lingua.2017.02.002

## Research role
Prior classification of where incongruity occurs and how it is resolved in jokes.

## Core idea
Jokes can be analyzed through relationships between incongruity and resolution, with multiple cases identified across a corpus of 500 jokes.

## Relevance
Supports the sequence:

```text
Order → Premise → Expectation → Deviation → Resolution
```

## Limitation / gap
The project requires an explicit machine-readable ontology for world knowledge, roles, social norms, and context rather than merely reusing descriptive categories.

## Project extension
Represent deviation along semantic, role, social, logical, contextual, emotional, and linguistic axes.
