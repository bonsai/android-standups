# 01 — Domains-Interaction Approach

## Citation
Hillson & Martin (1994), *What's so funny about that?: The domains-interaction approach as a model of incongruity and resolution in humor*.

## URL
https://experts.mcmaster.ca/scholarly-works/1791686

## Research role
Prior work treating humor-related incongruity as interaction and distance between semantic domains.

## Core idea
Different meaning domains interact in jokes, producing incongruity. Humor is associated with incongruity together with resolution.

## Method / evidence
Referenced here as an analysis of 250 jokes examining semantic-domain distance and humor evaluation.

## Relevance
Direct precedent for the project's attempt to quantify deviation.

## Limitation / gap
Semantic distance alone does not express what was expected, why it was expected, what changed, or in which direction the deviation occurred.

## Project extension
```text
World Knowledge → Order → Premise → Expectation → Deviation Vector → Humor
```

Research gap candidate: extend semantic distance into an explainable structured Deviation Vector.
