# Research Lab — Three Stages

The research process is deliberately divided into three stages.

```text
01-theme-hypothesis
        ↓
02-seminar
        ↓
03-planning
        ↓
Experiment / Evaluation
        ↓
Hypothesis Revision
```

## 01 — Theme / Hypothesis

Define the research theme, inspect prior work, build the ontology, identify the research gap, and formulate falsifiable hypotheses.

## 02 — Seminar

Run a conversational research seminar. Seniors challenge novelty, theory, NLP validity, cognition, statistics, domain validity, and experimental design. The professor performs the final research-quality review.

## 03 — Planning

Convert the seminar outcome into research questions, operational definitions, variables, datasets, baselines, evaluation metrics, falsification conditions, and an experiment specification.

## Trigger

A human writes a hypothesis. The Lab then proceeds through these three stages.

## Language policy

LLM-facing research files: English-only Markdown.
Human-facing records: Japanese meeting minutes / HTML-oriented outputs.
