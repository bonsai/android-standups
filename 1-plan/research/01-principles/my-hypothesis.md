# My Hypothesis

## Principle

Humor may arise when a recognizable expectation is violated by a measurable deviation that remains interpretable.

## Strong form

Humor may function as a detector of the boundary of an established order.

## Research target

Identify, represent, and measure the boundary between unexpectedness and humor.

## Core model

```text
World Knowledge
  -> Order
  -> Premise
  -> Expectation
  -> Expected / Actual
  -> Deviation Vector
  -> Interpretability
  -> Resolution
  -> Humor Judgment
```

## Key distinction

Unexpectedness is not equivalent to humor.

The research should investigate whether humor emerges within a measurable region where deviation is sufficiently surprising but remains interpretable and resolvable.

## Main engineering hypothesis

An explicit representation of expected state, actual state, and deviation may explain human humor judgments better than a simple semantic-distance score.

## Research questions

- Can humor-relevant deviations be explicitly represented?
- Can deviation be quantified across dimensions?
- When does deviation become humor rather than mere surprise?
- Can the representation explain human humor judgments?
- Can it improve humor generation after evaluation?

## Falsification

The hypothesis should be weakened or rejected if independent annotators cannot reliably identify deviations, structured deviation does not improve explanatory or predictive performance over simpler baselines, or the proposed model fails under controlled experiments.

## Status

Working hypothesis. Not an established result.
